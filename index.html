FAQs

Common Questions

How much does interior painting cost in NYC, Westchester, or Long Island?

Interior painting in New York City, Westchester County, and Long Island typically ranges from $2–$5 per square foot for walls, depending on surface condition, ceiling height, and paint quality. Drywall repair and prep work may add to the total. We offer free, no-obligation estimates — call (347) 335-1078 or fill out our quote form above.

Do you do drywall repair before painting?

Yes — proper drywall repair and surface prep is the foundation of a lasting paint job. We handle everything from small patches and skim coat to full panel replacement before any painting begins. This ensures a smooth, professional result every time.

What areas do you serve in New York?

We serve all five New York City boroughs (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island), all of Westchester County (Yonkers, White Plains, New Rochelle, Scarsdale, Mount Vernon, Tarrytown, Harrison, Ossining, and more), Long Island — including Nassau County (Hempstead, Garden City, Long Beach, Freeport) and Suffolk County (Huntington, Babylon, and beyond) — and nearby Connecticut border towns (Stamford, Greenwich). Call us to confirm we cover your specific location.

Can you remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes. Popcorn ceiling removal and smooth finishing is one of our most requested services. We safely scrape, skim coat, and repaint ceilings to give your home a modern, clean look. Contact us for a free estimate.

Do you offer commercial painting services?

Yes. We work with property managers, landlords, and business owners across Westchester County, New York City, and Long Island. We schedule around your business hours to minimize disruption and deliver clean, professional results.

Do you serve Long Island — Nassau County and Suffolk County?

Yes. We serve communities throughout Nassau County (Hempstead, Garden City, Long Beach, Freeport, Valley Stream) and Suffolk County (Huntington, Babylon, Bay Shore, Islip, and beyond). Call (347) 335-1078 to confirm availability for your area.

How long does an interior painting project take?

Most single-room projects are completed in one day. Full-apartment or multi-room jobs typically take two to four days, depending on prep work and drywall repairs needed. We provide a clear timeline with every free estimate.

Are you licensed and insured in New York?

Yes. Residential Finishes LLC is fully insured and carries general liability coverage for all work performed across New York City, Westchester County, and Long Island. Your home and property are protected on every job.
